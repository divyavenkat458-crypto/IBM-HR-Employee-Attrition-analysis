# 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset. The main objective is to understand the factors that influence employee turnover and identify patterns among employees who leave the organization.

The analysis was performed using MySQL to explore employee demographics, job-related factors, salary, overtime, job satisfaction, and other employee characteristics.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze the overall employee attrition rate.
- Identify departments with the highest employee attrition.
- Analyze the impact of overtime on employee attrition.
- Understand the relationship between salary and employee turnover.
- Analyze work-life balance and job satisfaction.
- Identify age groups with higher attrition.
- Analyze attrition based on job roles and employee demographics.
- Identify the profile of employees who are more likely to leave.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

**Employee Attrition**
Analyzed the overall number and rate of employees who left the organization.

**Department Analysis**
Compared employee attrition across different departments to identify departments experiencing higher turnover.

**Overtime Analysis**
Examined whether employees working overtime have a higher tendency to leave the organization.

**Salary Analysis**
Compared monthly income with employee attrition to understand whether salary may influence employee turnover.

**Age Group Analysis**
Analyzed employee attrition across different age groups to identify which groups have higher turnover.

**Job Role Analysis**
Identified job roles with higher numbers of employees leaving the organization.

**Job Satisfaction & Work-Life Balance**
Analyzed how job satisfaction and work-life balance are associated with employee attrition.

## 💡 Key Findings
The analysis focuses on identifying:
- Overall company attrition rate
- Whether overtime is a major attrition driver
- Which department loses the most employees
- Whether salary or work-life balance has a stronger relationship with attrition
- The profile of employees who are more likely to leave
